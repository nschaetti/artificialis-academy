# MATH210 · Methods in mathematics for modelling

**Subject** Maths  
**Tier** Intermediate

## Prerequisites

You should be comfortable with single-variable differentiation and integration, including the chain rule, product rule, substitution, and elementary exponential, logarithmic and trigonometric functions. The first unit opens with a short diagnostic. The unit introduces the complex-root notation it uses.

## Units

1. [First- and second-order differential equations](unit-01/main.typ) ([PDF](unit-01/MATH210-Unit-01.pdf)) · separation of variables, integrating factors, direction fields, Euler’s method, characteristic roots, and undetermined coefficients.
